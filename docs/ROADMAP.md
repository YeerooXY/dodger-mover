# Roadmap

The roadmap is intentionally hypothesis-driven. Later slices may change after playtests; their promises should remain stable only when supported by evidence.

## S000 — Autonomous foundation

Repository rules, architecture, CI guards, review prompts, asset budgeting, and a minimal tested combat domain.

## S001 — Impact proof

A five-minute graybox with movement, jumping, light/heavy/launcher attacks, one target enemy, hit-stop, knockback, recovery, and instant restart.

## S002 — Platform combat proof

Air follow-ups, directional attacks, ledges, drops, and two simultaneous enemy pressures. Tests whether traversal improves combat decisions.

## S003 — Flash fantasy vertical slice

One authored gauntlet with an initial visual identity, style scoring, complete feedback, and a ten-minute replayable arc.

## S004 — Demo production plan

The first full content plan is written only after S003 evidence. Candidate work includes progression, encounter variety, a boss, accessibility, content tools, and release packaging.
